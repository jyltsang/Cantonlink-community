# Contributing

Thank you for helping make free Cantonese learning material more reliable. The project is at the planning stage, so the most valuable contributions right now are **feedback on the [product specification](docs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md)** and **offers to review content**.

## Ways to help

- **Report an error.** Open an issue with the pattern or word, what is wrong and what you would suggest instead.
- **Review content.** If you are a native or near-native speaker of Hong Kong Cantonese, open an issue titled "Reviewer" — reviewers are credited by name.
- **Comment on the spec.** Open an issue or start a discussion about any section.

## Content standards

Content follows the quality process in [§8 of the spec](docs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md#8-content-quality--what-flawless-means). In short:

1. **Spoken Hong Kong Cantonese.** Where written Chinese differs, show both and label the written form.
2. **Jyutping is the source of truth.** Write tone numbers (`nei5 hou2`); Yale is generated automatically, so do not type it by hand.
3. **Hong Kong Traditional characters**, used consistently (嘅, 喺, 咗, 啲).
4. **Natural English**, not word-for-word glosses.
5. **Original work only.** Do not copy sentences from textbooks, dictionaries or websites unless their licence allows it; say where anything came from.
